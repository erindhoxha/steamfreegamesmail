What is Steam Free Games Mail?

Every so often a publisher gives a paid Steam game away for free. Claim it before the offer ends and it stays in your library forever. These offers have no fixed day and often last only a day or two, so they are easy to miss.

We check Steam every 30 minutes and send you an email when one appears. Free weekends, where you can play a game for free for a few days, are included too and clearly marked.
